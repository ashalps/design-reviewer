## Priority edits

### P01 — competing headline and badge

- **Where/evidence:** upper headline area, E1.
- **Before → after:** two equally strong messages → one clear event headline with a supporting promotion.
- **Why:** the viewer should understand the event before reading secondary details.
- **Priority/confidence/effort:** high / medium / small.
- **How:** in the original editable design, reduce the badge's visual emphasis and position it near the headline as supporting information. Adjust size/weight in the actual layout; no exact font size is inferred from this fictional preview.
- **Check:** at normal feed size, the event headline reads first, followed by product image and event details. Confirm with a viewer rather than claiming attention was measured.
- **Keep:** approved copy, logo, warm palette and product crop.

### P02 — date and booking details are difficult to read

- **Where/evidence:** bottom information block, E2.
- **Before → after:** small supporting text → a readable date followed by a clear booking action.
- **Priority/confidence/effort:** high / medium / small.
- **How:** group date and booking information; increase their size and separation while retaining safe margins. Use native editable text. Do not delete required details just to simplify the layout.
- **Check:** inspect the final export at intended display size for readability, clipping and overlaps.

## Editing order and unknowns

Fix P01, then P02, export again and inspect the actual final image. Check original dimensions, safe area and export quality before considering delivery verified. Do not claim that these edits were applied by issuing this review.
